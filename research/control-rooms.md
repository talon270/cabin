Confidence describes the source type: **primary** means an operator, railway authority, standard, or first-hand artifact; **secondary** means a trade or other secondary publication; **forum** means a forum; **unsourced** means I found no source establishing the claim.

## Conventions cabin should copy exactly

- **Show clear track unlit, set-and-locked routes white, and occupied track red even when a route is set.** IDs: IN-01, IN-02.
- **Show point position, signal aspects and route condition as explicit operating information.** IDs: IN-03, IN-07.
- **Make the complete yard viewable, allow enlargement, and support entrance-exit route setting across screens if the yard is split.** IDs: IN-06, DR-02.
- **Provide system/communication health and track-section status.** IDs: IN-05, IN-07.
- **Start practice with layout, routes and signals, then introduce procedures and scenario changes before failures and supervised local work.** IDs: TR-01, TR-06, TRN-01, TRN-03, TRN-05.

## Conflicts between sources

- **Indian Railways versus TTC photo:** Indian Railways sources specify an unlit clear track and white (or yellow/white in the broader track-detection wording) for an unoccupied set route, with red occupied track. The TTC photo shows pale-blue track and green runs, but its semantic colour key is not published. Treating those TTC colours as Indian Railways indications is unsupported. IDs: IN-01, IN-02, TR-05.
- **Indian Railways versus the UK examples:** the UK sources document dark-background workstations and one Controlguide example with white route and red occupancy. They do not establish a current UK-wide palette, so they cannot resolve or supersede the Indian Railways manual. IDs: UK-02, UK-03, UK-05, UK-06.
- **RDSO versus one-screen layouts:** the big-yard RDSO specification allows multiple monitors and requires cross-screen entrance-exit route setting. A one-screen-per-station drawing is a design choice, not a requirement of that source. IDs: IN-06, DR-06.
- **Exact geometry remains open:** examples show horizontal schematic tracks, but the reviewed sources do not specify track spacing, label placement or a universal 45-degree crossover angle. IDs: DR-03, DR-04, DR-05.
