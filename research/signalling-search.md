## Verdict

No positions of real NDLS signals were found, so the game's signals stay generated by rule and tagged `authored` (plan A3). The nearest useful facts are 16 platforms with 1 and 16 at the two ends (S-01, S-02), which is the same ordering `cabin-osm` assumes when it numbers platform tracks west to east, and an interlocking size (S-04) that the game must not print as a record claim (plan A8).

**S-04 and S-05 disagree on whose interlocking has 1,122.** The movement count in S-04 and the route count in S-05 are the same number but are attached to different stations. Neither is used as a game value.
